# Client Proposal

Every proposal follows one fixed structure so clients always know where to look. Keep it short: the system map and the mapping session recording carry the detail, and the proposal carries the decision.

## Inputs to gather first

Before drafting, collect what exists and read it:

1. **Mapping session** — recording, transcript or notes. The main source for challenges, outcomes and scope.
2. **System map** — the visual map of the system to be built (link only; don't redescribe it in full).
3. **Client documentation** — briefs, workflow docs, email threads, existing CRM exports.
4. **Pricing** — the paid-in-full price, the payment plan (usually higher in total) and its schedule.

Never invent facts, figures, prices or dates. Anything not confirmed by a source goes in `[square brackets]` as a placeholder, and the open questions go in the prep checklist at the top.

## Output structure

Follow `assets/template.md` exactly. In summary:

**Prep checklist (internal, deleted before sending)**: open questions to settle on the next call, plus reminders to link the map and recording.

**Cover**: project name, prepared for (names and roles), prepared by, date, valid-until date, and a table of supporting materials (system map, mapping session recording, client documentation).

**1. The Goal**
- One-sentence summary of what the project achieves for the client.
- *Where you are now*: 3–5 current challenges, in the client's own words where possible.
- *Where you want to be*: 3–5 specific, ideally measurable, desired outcomes.
- *What success looks like*: one short paragraph describing the business a few months after launch.

**2. The Plan**
- One sentence tying the plan to the map.
- *Key deliverables*: table with two columns, Deliverable and What it does for you. Write the benefits, not the features.
- *Milestones*: numbered list running from kick-off and access, through the build milestones and testing and launch, to handover and support. Give each milestone a week range.
- *Not included*: out-of-scope items discussed, so expectations are clear (optional).

**3. The Investment**
- *Cost*: table with Paid in full, Payment plan, and any optional add-ons. State the currency and whether VAT or tax is included. Note what third-party fees (platform subscription, SMS carrier fees) the client pays directly.
- *Timeframe*: expected start, expected launch, support period. Note any dependencies that could move dates.
- *What we need from you*: always include ongoing needs (regular communication and timely feedback, a single point of contact, attendance at reviews). Add a table of hard deliverables with needed-by dates when there are any.
- *Next steps*: confirm payment option, then agreement and invoice, then kick-off call. Restate the valid-until date.

## Style

- British spelling (organisation, prioritise, programme) for UK clients and by default. Use the client's currency.
- Speak to the client as "you", and to the agency as "we".
- Plain, confident language. Short sentences. No hype, jargon or guarantees of results.
- Every section should fit on roughly one screen.
- The first sentence of each section states its point.

## Output format

Produce the proposal as a shareable document in whatever the environment supports: a living doc, Google Doc or Notion page, or Markdown if nothing else is available. Keep the headings exactly as in the template so every proposal looks the same.
